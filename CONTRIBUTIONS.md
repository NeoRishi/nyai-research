# Contributions and AI assistance

This is my account of the work I have contributed to NyAI and related projects. It is an applicant statement, not an independent verification of my biography or experimental findings.

## My contributions

I conceived NyAI in March 2026 while building NeoRishi and exploring how to make AI-generated recommendations transparent, evidence-based and open to challenge. Drawing on my study of YogaShastra and Indian Knowledge Systems, I proposed using selected Nyaya ideas about justified knowledge, flawed inference and five-part reasoning as conceptual tools. I do not claim these concepts automatically improve AI reliability.

I directed the design of the original Python, FastAPI and React NyAI prototype with substantial AI coding assistance. The prototype explored source classification, fallacy checks, belief revision and inspectable reasoning traces. I developed it in connection with IIT Delhi's Unriddling Inference 2026 and proposed temporal-staleness checking as a practical adaptation, not an established additional classical fallacy. The five constructed demonstrations do not establish general reliability.

I created and maintained a private Markdown and Obsidian knowledge base and directed and worked on a Python and Neo4j GraphRAG proof of concept with AI coding assistance. I recognised limitations involving broad source categories, ambiguous concept relationships, fixed evidence weights and the difference between a valid source citation and a justified conclusion.

I chose to consolidate the earlier work into NyAI Research and pivot its direction from explanation quality towards checkable agent claims, actual execution evidence and changing dependencies.

For the recent studies, I set the objectives, reviewed proposed approaches and reported results, and assessed their relevance to AI safety. I do not claim to have personally implemented all code, independently created every case or manually executed every experiment.

## AI assistance and verification limits

ChatGPT, Claude and Codex substantially assisted with implementation, synthetic test cases, experiment orchestration and execution, analysis, and documentation. AI-generated literature critiques are not independent human peer review.

I have reviewed reported negative findings and limitations, but have not independently reproduced every run. Synthetic reference judgments have not received independent human validation, and human oversight benefits have not been established.

This record does not claim referee endorsement, independent expert validation, external participant studies or unassisted authorship of the implementation.
