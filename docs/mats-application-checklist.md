## Official requirements and the 90-day distinction

The published Bridge requirements ask for a research plan/theory of change, Why Bridge, and prior experience/future contribution. General materials include a maximum two-page CV, two references, one representative work sample, profile/logistics, track choice, written responses and AI disclosure. The explicit 90-day-plan requirement is listed under Incubation, not Bridge. We can include a 90-day validation phase in the Bridge proposal; it is proposed future work, not a claim of completed validation.

The live form stresses substantial research-calibre work for which the applicant was meaningfully responsible. NyAI's current artifact is preliminary; present its negative/ceiling results and assisted development accurately. A polished repository does not itself establish readiness or fit.

## Proposed first 90 days of research

These are proposed milestones, not official Bridge requirements or guaranteed outcomes.

| Period | Work | Inspectable deliverable and decision |
|---|---|---|
| Days 1-30 | Define the threat model; author 30-40 varied cases with source excerpts; obtain two reviewers' labels where feasible; freeze the rubric and analysis | Versioned benchmark, reviewer agreement and unresolved ambiguities. Revise the rubric before making performance claims if labels are unreliable. |
| Days 31-60 | Compare strong model baselines, a matched generic checklist, structured records and executable checks with equal evidence access; begin trusted runtime observations | Reproducible paired results, invalid-output accounting, false-positive/false-negative analysis and latency. Separate retrieval effects from reasoning effects. |
| Days 61-90 | Subject to recruitment, consent and appropriate review, pilot human error detection and review effort; test correction and adversarial traces | A report on appropriate reliance, review burden and failure modes. Simplify or pivot if the mechanism adds burden without a useful detection benefit. |

After the initial phase, extend promising mechanisms to longer agent trajectories, independently authored domains, stronger adversaries and replicated reviewer studies. Success means a reliable answer about usefulness and limits, including a negative result; it does not mean proving general AI safety within 90 days. Freeze numerical success thresholds and a feasible study design before the confirmatory comparison.
