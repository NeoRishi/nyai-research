# From reproducible prototype to a stronger research work sample

As of 26 October 2026, the repository supports a preliminary research work sample. It does not establish improved human oversight, general reasoning superiority or safety of autonomous research agents. The goal is a defensible contribution, not a larger product.

## Implemented now

- Real GraphRAG experiments, raw responses, scoring and limitations are preserved.
- A new observed-execution diagnostic runs actual tests and binds decisions to artifact/task/execution identity. It exercises revision, correction and loss of authority.
- The meaningful conventional workflow baseline ties NyAI. This negative incremental finding is stated explicitly.
- A review packet and empty worksheet allow a person to challenge scenario judgments.
- No framework migration, private-corpus upload, new paid model run or public publication is needed for these improvements.

## Highest-value next work

1. **Applicant understanding and research ownership.** Inspect the evidence, reproduce the diagnostic, challenge at least one design decision and write what changed your mind. Record actual contributions rather than adopting assistant-generated reasoning as personal work. This step cannot be completed by the assistant on the applicant's behalf.
2. **Independent task and label review.** Ask a technically qualified reviewer to examine the policy and review packet before seeing our expected decisions. Record ambiguity. Ask a reviewer to author additional cases without copying our event templates. Neither another AI pass nor applicant review is independent external validation. No reviewer contact has been authorized or sent.
3. **A harder, task-grounded benchmark.** Use a modest set of distinct research task families: stale evaluation artifacts; changed dataset splits; unsupported generalization from metrics; incomplete test execution; contradictory reruns. Each needs a checkable reference artifact and a clear statement of what a test can and cannot establish. Include ordinary successes and appropriate abstentions. Hold out entire task families, not near-duplicate variants.
4. **Fair live-model experiment.** Freeze cases, labels, model conditions, failure handling and evidence budgets. Compare direct evidence with a conventional guarded workflow and the same workflow with explicit warrant/revision records. Use more than one draw/model where the budget permits. Obtain approval for any new paid API scope before running it; earlier approvals do not silently authorize this new experiment. Keep private KB content out of payloads.
5. **Reviewer benefit.** If participants are available, assign comparable tasks to ordinary logs versus explicit records, counterbalance task order and measure review errors and time. Do not claim a pilot proves general oversight benefit. Avoid rushing an underpowered human study just to include it in the application.

Framework choice follows the experiment. LangGraph may help manage execution, state and human intervention; Neo4j remains the evidence graph. The proposed contribution must outperform or clarify something beyond the framework's ordinary guards. LangChain adapters and CrewAI collaboration are optional and currently unnecessary.

## Optional first 90 days of research

- Days 1–30: prior-art review, task/rubric development with external review, trusted artifact capture and frozen baseline protocol.
- Days 31–60: held-out live-model comparisons, error analysis and ablations of evidence binding, explicit defeaters and revision records.
- Days 61–90: feasible reviewer study, replication on a second task family/model and a report that states positive or negative findings without selective reporting.

These are proposed research milestones, not completed results, guaranteed outcomes or an assertion that Bridge requires a 90-day plan. The explicit 90-day application requirement identified earlier belongs to Incubation.
